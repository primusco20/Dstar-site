Questions

Can I use any branch?

Yes. One membership covers every Dstars branch, every day.

Can I freeze my membership?

Up to 60 days a year. Tell the front desk and we'll pause it from that day.

What happens when it expires?

Access stops, but your training history stays. Renew whenever you're ready and pick up where you left off.

Is there a joining fee?

No. You pay for the term and nothing else.

How old do I need to be?

Dstars membership is 18 and over.

Do I need to book?

Not to train. Walk in during opening hours. Coaching sessions are booked with your coach.
